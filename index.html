About Our Heritage

Devoted exclusively to Maa Saraswati & Community Upliftment

Paniparul New K.B.S Club stands as a beacon of unity, culture, and sacred devotion. Unlike multi-festive organizations, our club is uniquely dedicated to organizing Sri Sri Saraswati Puja every year with utmost reverence, grandeur, and traditional purity.

Our mission is to foster knowledge, promote local artistic talent, support youth development, and bring together families across generations under the divine blessings of the Goddess of Wisdom.
